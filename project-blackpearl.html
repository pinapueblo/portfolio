What I learned

Engineering lessons from the full design cycle

Design for Manufacturing

A successful design is not finished when the CAD model works; it also needs to be efficient to manufacture and assemble.

Design Consolidation

Reducing unnecessary components can simplify manufacturing, assembly, sourcing, and maintenance.

Requirements-Driven Engineering

Engineering decisions must balance functional requirements, environmental constraints, manufacturability, and system-level considerations.

Iteration

Moving from CAD to prototype to production revealed opportunities that were not obvious during the initial design phase.
