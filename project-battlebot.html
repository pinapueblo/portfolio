Mini Battle Bot

A compact combat robot designed and built for small-scale robotics competitions. This project combines mechanical engineering, electronics integration, and strategy optimization to create an efficient fighting machine. From chassis design to weapon system integration, every component was engineered for maximum performance within strict weight and size constraints.

Engineering Highlights

Project Objective

  • Design and manufacture a competitive combat robot under 1 lb.
  • Integrate weapon systems for maximum damage output and reliability.
  • Optimize drive systems for agility and controlled movement.

My Role

  • Led chassis design and material selection in SOLIDWORKS.
  • Designed and integrated motor mounts and weapon drive systems.
  • Conducted FEA analysis for structural durability under combat conditions.
  • Coordinated electronics and control system integration.

Key Features

  • Lightweight aluminum chassis with reinforced stress points.
  • Dual-motor drive system for precise control and maneuverability.
  • High-RPM weapon system with optimized power distribution.
  • Custom-designed motor mounts for vibration isolation.

Results

  • Achieved rapid assembly and reliable operation across multiple matches.
  • Demonstrated consistent weapon performance throughout competition season.
  • Successful integration of all subsystems with minimal weight overhead.

Chassis Design & CAD

The foundation of a competitive bot starts with a well-thought-out chassis. Careful CAD work ensured every component fit perfectly while maintaining structural integrity under combat stress.

Weapon System Engineering

The weapon system is the heart of any combat robot. Custom motor mounts and power distribution were engineered to maximize impact while keeping the system balanced and reliable.

Drive System & Motors

Dual brushless motors provide responsive control and impressive acceleration. The drive system was tuned for both raw power and precision maneuvering in arena conditions.

  • 1 × 280 DC motor for the spinner
  • 1 × DC servo motor for the arm
  • 2 × DC gear motors for drive

Assembly & Testing

Bringing everything together required careful coordination and extensive testing to ensure all subsystems worked in harmony.
